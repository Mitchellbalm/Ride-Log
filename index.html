Drag and drop one or many activity files from your Garmin Connect export. Everything is parsed locally in your browser — no upload, no account, your data stays on your device.

Getting your files a short guide

Garmin Connect → profile picture → Account Information → Manage Your Data → Export Your Data. You'll get an email with a ZIP. Unzip and look for files named *_ACTIVITY.fit — those are your rides (anything _WELLNESS or _METRICS is daily stats, not what we want). For individual rides going forward, use Exporteer bestand in the activity menu.
