Drop your FIT files here

Drag and drop one or many .fit files from your Garmin Connect export. Everything is parsed locally in your browser — no upload, no account, your data stays on your device.

Getting your files

Log in to Garmin Connect → profile picture → Account Information → Manage Your Data → Export Your Data. You'll get an email with a ZIP. Unzip it and drop the .fit files above. For individual rides going forward, use the "Exporteer bestand" option in the activity menu.
